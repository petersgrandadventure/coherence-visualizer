How to read the field

Every second each egg — a physical noise source on this Mac — produces one 200-bit trial, scored as a z-value against its own empirical calibration. The field turns those z-values, their network combination, and the running cumulative statistic into motion, so the state of the instrument can be read at a glance, with the control beside it.

Families and rings

  • mic Microphone / ADC thermal noise. Inner rings, one per physical egg, assigned in the order the eggs appear. The name sits at each ring's 12 o'clock so identity never depends on colour alone.
  • accel Accelerometer LSB parity.
  • camera Image-sensor noise through the ISP.
  • control (ghost) /dev/random, run through byte-identical code. The sparse grey family on the outermost inner ring. It never changes colour and ignores coherence — it is what chance looks like on this machine.

Breathing = each egg's z

A ring's radius breathes with its egg's z-value (eased over ~5 s): outward for positive z, inward for negative, ±10 % per σ, capped at ±3σ. Particle brightness follows |z|. A stale egg (no data for more than 10 s, or a simulated fault) dims to a quarter and its card gets a stale tag.

Convergence = coherence drive

From the 10-minute window the page takes s = max(|Stouffer Z|, netvar z) and maps it to a drive C = smoothstep(1, 3, s). At C = 0 every family drifts at its own speed and direction in its identity hue; as C → 1 the families co-rotate at one shared speed and their hues converge toward gold, with more glow. The control ghost ignores C entirely — if the grey ring starts to look like the others, that is a bug, not a signal.

The outer ring = the GCP cumulative-deviation plot, bent into a circle

Starting at 12 o'clock and running clockwise over the display window, the trace is the network's cumulative deviation — by default the network variance Σ(Zt²−1), or, with the toggle, the mean shift Σz. Its radial offset is the value divided by the 95 % band half-width at that sample count, so the translucent annulus is the 95 % band and the faint dashed rings are the 99 % limits. Where the trace leaves the 95 % band it turns gold. The thin grey line is the control egg's own cumulative trace on the same scale. The bright dot is now.

The band is simultaneous: its constants come from bootstrap paths of the control egg's own z-scores, chosen so 95 % (99 %) of whole paths stay inside for every step. The classic pointwise parabola (±1.96√n) is exited by about half of all purely random paths somewhere along their length — a display that draws it will cry wolf every other window. Nothing is drawn before 30 samples.

Status levels

  • CHANCE-LIKE Nothing departs from the control's behaviour.
  • ATTENTION The trace head is outside the 95 % band, or the 10-minute statistic has p < 0.05 (empirical vs control history in LIVE, analytic otherwise).
  • EXCURSION (pulsing) Outside the 99 % band, or p < 0.01.
  • INSTRUMENT A physical egg is stale, calibration is theoretical, an egg helper restarted during this session, the endpoint failed three polls, or the server's data is older than 30 s. Statistics are not to be trusted until this clears.

Modes

Live polls reg_bridge.py (/api/state?window=W) every 2 s; tail probabilities are empirical — the fraction of independent windows, bootstrapped from the control egg's own z-history, at least as extreme (never reported as 0; floored at one over the null's size). Only eggs with empirical constants that agree with their canonical trial are scored; the rest are listed as excluded. Simulation runs synthetic Binomial(200, ½) eggs with injectable effects (each lasts 5 min): a mean shift, a variance inflation, correlated eggs (what network variance is built to detect), and an instrument fault; p-values there are analytic. History replays the bridge's per-second record; scrubbing sets now and everything is recomputed from the z arrays up to that moment. Gold ticks in the strip mark where the 10-minute |Stouffer Z| exceeded 1.96.

An honest note: the GCP's published effect is ~0.3σ per pre-registered event, pooled across ~60 devices. A three-egg home instrument cannot detect an effect that size. This page's job is narrower and more useful: to show, against a defined null and with the control drawn beside it, whether this field ever departs from chance — and to say so in numbers.
